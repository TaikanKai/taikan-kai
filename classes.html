Classes

Supportive karate training for children, teens and adults.

Train practical skills, confidence, coordination, resilience and body awareness.

Kids Class

Tuesday, 4:30–5:30 PM

A shorter, supportive class for children. Students learn karate through movement, skill-based games and focused drills that build confidence, coordination and focus.

Perfect for beginners. No experience needed. Parents are welcome to stay and watch.

All Levels Class

Tuesday, 6:00–7:30 PM

Mixed class for beginners and experienced students. Covers foundations, kata, partner work, self-defence, movement and confidence-building.

Graded Students Class

By invitation / graded students

Focused training for students preparing for higher grade material, deeper kata work and more advanced application.

What to expect

Warm-up and movement

Mobility, coordination, balance and body control.

Karate skills

Strikes, blocks, stances, footwork, kata and combinations.

Practical application

Partner drills, boundaries, awareness and realistic self-defence concepts.
