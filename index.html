About the style

Embodied understanding through martial arts.

Taikan Kai means “Association of Embodied Understanding.” Our training is built around awareness, adaptability, discipline and learning to stay calm under pressure.

We respect traditional martial arts while making training accessible, meaningful and useful for modern life.
